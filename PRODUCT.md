# Product

## Register

product

(The homepage, use-case pages, and other marketing surfaces run in the **brand** register; the rest of the app — search, garden pages, dashboards, publishing flows — is product UI. Design work on `/` should be treated as brand.)

## Users

Computational scientists and research software engineers — materials science, chemistry, biology, physics — who want to find, run, adapt, and publish specialized AI models without fighting environments or infrastructure. They arrive from papers, conference talks, docs links, and collaborators; many hold HPC allocations (ALCF, NERSC, NCSA) or use cloud GPU quotas. Secondary audiences: model authors seeking users and citations, and program officers / institutional partners evaluating the project's credibility.

## Product Purpose

Garden (thegardens.ai) is an open, NSF-funded platform for discovering, running, and sharing citable AI models for science. Models are published in "Gardens" with DOIs, pre-bundled containerized runtimes, and on-demand execution on cloud (Modal) or HPC (Globus Compute). Success: a researcher goes from finding a model to a real prediction in minutes, and model authors get real users and citations. Adjacent efforts: the Almanac of Matter Models (registry of MLIPs pre-installed on HPC clusters) and Rootstock (one ASE-compatible calculator to run any of them).

## Brand Personality

Credible, precise, quietly ambitious. This is national-lab-adjacent scientific infrastructure, not a SaaS startup: the design should convey importance without being overbearing, and confidence without hype. Voice is plainspoken and a little wry ("Science needs more than chatbots"). Emotional goals: trust, competence, "these people run things on supercomputers."

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-startup landing pages: gradient text, glassmorphism, hero-metric stat rows, identical icon-card grids, breathless copy about "revolutionizing science."
- Anything that reads as "AI made that" — template scaffolding, eyebrow labels above every section, stock 3D blobs.
- Overbearing gravitas: full-screen manifesto typography, dramatic scroll-jacking. The work is important; the page shouldn't strain to say so.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the working product** — real models, real DOIs, real code snippets that run. Evidence over adjectives.
2. **Institutional credibility, modern execution** — dark-slate/teal system, disciplined typography; polish signals engineering quality.
3. **Respect the reader's expertise** — scientists skim; lead with what it does and how to use it, not marketing framing.
4. **Quiet confidence** — one strong accent (teal, lime for highlights), restrained motion, no shouting.
5. **Every section earns its place** — cut scaffolding; a section exists because a researcher needs its information.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA as the working bar: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥3:1, visible focus states, reduced-motion alternatives for any animation. Audience includes color-vision-deficient users; never encode meaning in color alone.
